Webflow

Teva’s Mental Health Report 2025 presents the mental health reality in Israel following October 7. Despite the alarming statistics, I created a concept with a message of growth. From sunset to sunrise, from grief to hope for the future, through Teva’s brand.

I designed and developed an animated landing page that highlights key statistics as you scroll from dark to light, and leads you to the fully designed report.

Does the desire to marry stem from love, social pressure or maybe out of fantasy? And what happens when the fantasy is shattered?

"The Perfect Match" is a futuristic dystopian comic that explores the social pressures surrounding marriage and children. The story follows a young girl who joins a program promising to find her "the one" and to live the stereotypical happy life.

Nominated for the 2025 Cartes Award for Comics and Graphic Novels.
